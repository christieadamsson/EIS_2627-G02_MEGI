Actosr: 
Business client: 
- has a login 
- registers product (5kg, 20kg package, small parcel etc)
- places order (if online)
- Enter the address/destination of the recipient (Backend automatically fetches lat & long of origin)
- pays via card, apple/google pay, paypal
- receives an invoice order/order confirmation
- access the status of active order:
- receives a tracking number from courier company
- types in tracking number on the website
- sees status

Recipient client: 
- access the status of active order:
- receives a tracking number from courier company
- types in tracking number on the website
- sees status
- access delivery
- have parcel delivered to door
- order complete
- (else if not at home), have parcel delivered to shop
- go to parcel shop to pick up
- order complete

Courier: (1 or more vehicles)
- has a login
- receives orders
- chooses whether one or multiple orders in the route
- select appropriate vehicle for the order
- executes order
- when delivered submit Electronic Proof of Delivery (digital Canvas signature or a delivery photo)
- OR if cancelled order returns to the list of orders 
- view completed orders

Staff:
- In charge of system governance
- Validate courier accounts
- Account approvals
- Vehicle inspection and insurance validation

Route operations (Operations Manager)
- local distribution
- daily transport planning
- fleet management (how many trucks?)
- Oversee vehicle routes 
- Update vehicle whereabouts


Customer service
- receives complaint
- serves customer
